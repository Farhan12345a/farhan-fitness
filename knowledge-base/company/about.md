# About Farhan Fitness

## Company Overview

**Farhan Fitness LLC** is an independent strength and conditioning gym headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2019 by Farhan Shahbaz, the facility has grown from a single studio into a neighborhood hub for barbell training, conditioning classes, and personal coaching.

## Mission Statement

To help every member move better, get stronger, and build sustainable training habits through expert coaching, honest programming, and a welcoming community.

## Vision

To be the most trusted neighborhood gym for busy professionals and athletes who want structured training without the gimmicks.

## Core Values

- **Coaching First**: Every member deserves clear guidance and feedback
- **Consistency Over Hype**: Simple, repeatable systems beat novelty for novelty's sake
- **Respect the Floor**: Rerack your weights, wipe down equipment, support others
- **Progressive Overload**: Small improvements compound over months and years
- **Inclusion**: All experience levels are welcome on a judged-by-effort basis
