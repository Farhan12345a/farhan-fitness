# Farhan Fitness — Lead Coaches (Specialist Roles)

## Coaching Team Overview

Farhan Fitness employs 14 coaches across group classes, personal training, and onboarding. This document mirrors specialist flight-crew structure using **lead coach** roles (mock org chart).

## Coach Levels (Mock)

### Head Coach (Floor Captain)
**Requirements:**
- 5+ years coaching experience
- Advanced barbell and injury-reduction coursework (mock internal levels)
- Mentorship responsibility for two junior coaches

**Responsibilities:**
- Final say on floor safety during peak blocks
- Program alignment with Director of Coaching
- Member escalation on training disputes

### Senior Coach
**Requirements:**
- 3+ years experience
- Comfortable leading 16-person classes alone

**Responsibilities:**
- Class delivery and scaling options
- New member movement screens (mock 15-minute protocol)

### Associate Coach
**Requirements:**
- 1+ year experience or completed apprenticeship
- CPR/AED current

**Responsibilities:**
- Assist senior coach on busy classes
- Warm-up delivery and equipment setup

## Specialty Assignments (Mock)

### Olympic Lifting Focus Coaches
- 3 senior coaches
- Typical sessions: Saturday barbell club, open platform hours

### Conditioning Leads
- 4 coaches rotating **Engine Room** classes
- Equipment: bikes, rowers, ski ergs (mock inventory)

### Mobility / Recovery Leads
- 2 coaches
- Typical sessions: Sunday **Mobility Reset**
