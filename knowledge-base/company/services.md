# Farhan Fitness Services

## Membership Options

### Access Tiers

#### All-Access
- Unlimited open gym during published hours
- Unlimited group classes (capacity permitting)
- One complimentary form check session per quarter (mock)
- Guest pass: 1 per month

#### Hybrid
- 12 visits per month (open gym or class; combined pool)
- Priority booking for weekend strength blocks
- Standard locker access

#### Class Pass Pack (Non-member)
- 10-class bundle, valid 90 days from purchase
- No open-gym access except 15 minutes before/after class

### Group Classes (Mock Schedule Topics)

- **Barbell Basics**: Squat, press, deadlift patterning for newer lifters
- **Engine Room**: Interval work on bikes, rowers, and runs
- **Strong Saturday**: Longer strength-focused session with partner work
- **Mobility Reset**: Low-intensity movement and breathing (mock)

### Special Services

- **Personal Training**: 30- or 60-minute sessions booked via app or front desk
- **Teen Strength (13–17)**: Parent waiver required; mock summer cohort
- **Nutrition Office Hours**: Monthly Q&A with a partner dietitian (illustrative)
- **Equipment Orientation**: Complimentary 20-minute tour for new members
